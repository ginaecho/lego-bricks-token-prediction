# Freedom of information request handling

Around 400 FOI requests a month, each with a statutory 20-working-day deadline
the agency misses about a third of the time.

Per request: read the request and work out what is actually being asked for,
retrieve the candidate documents across the records system, review each
candidate for relevance, classify any exemptions that might apply, and draft the
response letter with the disclosure log entry.

Exemption decisions are made by the information governance team, not by the
pipeline. What we are automating is the search and the first pass, which is
where the twenty days go.

Candidate document sets vary wildly — call it 120 kB per request on average.
Compliance driven: the deadline is statutory and the misses are reportable.
