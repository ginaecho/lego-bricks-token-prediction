# Well permit pack preparation

Relecloud's regulatory affairs team assembles permit application packs. Each pack
takes about three weeks of one person's time and the regulator returns roughly
40% of them for incomplete documentation.

Per application: retrieve the required documents across the technical records,
review each for currency and applicability, extract the data points the
application form needs, validate the assembled pack against the regulator's
completeness checklist, and draft the covering submission.

About 25 applications a year. Technical records per application are large,
around 400 kB.

The client will justify this on the returned-pack rate rather than on the three
weeks, because the returns delay drilling and that is where the money is.

Team: an industry consultant who knows the regulator's checklist is
non-negotiable. The rest is open.
