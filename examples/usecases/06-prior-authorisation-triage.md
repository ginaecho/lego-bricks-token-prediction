# Prior authorisation triage

Lamna Health processes prior-authorisation requests from providers. Volume is
about 40,000 a month and the service-level agreement is 48 hours; they are
missing it roughly a fifth of the time and each miss is a complaint.

Per request: read the submitted clinical note and the attached documentation,
classify the request against the plan's coverage categories, retrieve the
relevant policy clause from the coverage manual, and check the submission for
the three completeness criteria that cause most of the rejections.

Approvals still require a clinician. What we are automating is the triage and
the completeness check, so that the clinician sees a prepared file rather than a
raw submission.

Clinical notes average around 6 kB. Regulated environment, clinical safety
sign-off required, and the client's information governance team will want to
review the whole thing.
