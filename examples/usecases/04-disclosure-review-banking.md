# Quarterly disclosure review

Every quarter Woodgrove's reporting team assembles the disclosure pack and a
reviewer works through the regulator's checklist line by line. It takes two
senior people about a week and it always lands on the last possible day.

We want the pipeline to read the six documents in the pack, extract the reported
figures, reconcile each of them to the filed accounts, write the twelve checks
the regulator's checklist implies, and produce a reviewer's note listing every
exception with the page reference.

This runs four times a year — it is not a volume play. The value is that the
review finishes a week earlier and the exceptions are found before the audit
committee sees the pack rather than during. Total pack is around 48 kB of text.
