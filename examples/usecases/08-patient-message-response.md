# Patient portal message drafting

Patients send about 55,000 messages a month through Lamna's portal. Most are
appointment logistics, repeat prescriptions or results questions. Clinical staff
answer them between appointments and the median response time is four days.

For each inbound message: classify it into the routing categories, retrieve the
relevant information from the patient record and the practice's information
pages, and draft a response for a member of staff to review and send.

Nothing goes out unreviewed. That is a hard constraint from the client's
clinical governance committee and it should be visible in the design, not a
footnote.

Messages are short, under 1 kB with the retrieved context. The business case is
patient experience and staff retention, not headcount.
