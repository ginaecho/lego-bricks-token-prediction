# Contoso service ticket triage

Contoso is a retail group. Their customer service desk takes around 22,000
tickets a week and triage is done by a first-line team who mostly read the
ticket and move it somewhere else.

Read the inbound ticket, classify it into the routing categories and the
priority bands, retrieve the customer's recent order and returns history, and
draft the first response for the queue owner to approve.

Straightforward volume automation. Tickets are short — with the retrieved
history, well under 2 kB. The client is measuring first-response time and cost
per ticket.
