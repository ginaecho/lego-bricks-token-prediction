# Product description generation

Contoso onboards around 4,000 new SKUs a month from suppliers and the product
copy is written by an agency at considerable expense, arriving two weeks after
the product could have gone live.

Per SKU: read the supplier's specification sheet, extract the attributes that
belong on the product page, and draft the short description, the long
description and the five bullet points the template wants.

Merchandising approves before anything publishes.

Specification sheets are small, about 3 kB. This is revenue growth — the
argument is time-to-live, not the agency fee, though the agency fee is what will
be quoted back at us.

Team: not proposed yet. Merchandising will review everything, so somebody has
to own that workflow with them.
