# Benefits claim first assessment

The agency receives about 30,000 benefit claims a month. Each is assessed
against the eligibility rules by a case officer, and the current backlog is
eleven weeks.

For each claim: read the application and the supporting evidence, extract the
declared income, household composition and residency details, classify the claim
against the eligibility categories, and run the seven completeness and
consistency checks that account for most of the returns to the applicant.

A case officer makes every decision. The pipeline prepares the file and flags
what is missing — it does not decide entitlement, and any suggestion that it
might will end the conversation with the department.

Claim packs are around 15 kB. Full audit trail required on every step,
accessibility standards apply, and the assurance process will involve the
department's digital service standard assessment.
