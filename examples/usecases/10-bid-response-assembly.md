# Tender response assembly

Tailspin manufactures industrial pumps and bids for about 40 large
tenders a year, winning nine. The bid team says
the binding constraint is not price, it is that they cannot answer the
questionnaires fast enough to bid for everything they could.

Per tender the pipeline should read the tender pack, classify each of the
roughly 80 questions against the answer library, retrieve the best previous
answer for each, draft a tailored response, and validate the finished pack
against the tender's compliance requirements — word limits, mandatory
attachments, certifications in date.

The bid manager rewrites whatever they want to rewrite. Tender packs are large,
around 250 kB.

Revenue growth. The number that matters to the client is bids submitted per
quarter, and they will judge this on whether that goes up.
